# Case Study Patterns

These are pattern references, not recipes. Re-verify current facts before relying on any named product commercially.

## Mature-category execution

Examples studied: Tarsi, Screen Studio, Xnapper, LookAway.

Transferable question:
> Is the category crowded because the job is valuable, while the existing experience still leaves room for a meaningfully preferred product?

Do not infer that polish alone is enough.

## Delight and enthusiast affinity

Example studied: Keeby.

Transferable question:
> Is there a community that already cares deeply about a sensory, expressive, or identity-related experience that software can make more satisfying?

## Personality integrated with function

Example studied: Clucky.

Transferable question:
> Does the product's personality reinforce its mechanism, or is branding merely decorative?

## Timing-sensitive launch

Example studied: Bendy.

Pattern:
`preparedness + platform/cultural trigger + analogy + very fast build + immediate distribution`

Transferable question:
> Is the upside large enough relative to a tightly capped build to justify a short commercial half-life?

## Outcome-oriented AI

Example studied: Josie AI.

Transferable question:
> Can the user describe the desired life/work outcome without mentioning AI? If not, the product may be selling technology instead of value.

## Output-as-distribution

Example studied: Screen Studio.

Transferable question:
> Does normal product use create an artifact that demonstrates the product's value to other potential users?

## Recipient loop

Example studied: Letter4U.

Transferable question:
> Does a user's output naturally reach another person who may become aware of or interested in the product?

## Fix-the-annoyance

Example studied: LookAway.

Transferable question:
> Do users already want the category but abandon existing products because of one recurring irritation?

## Domain/local workflow insight

Example studied: Kape Tools.

Transferable question:
> Does the builder understand fragmented behavior, culture, paperwork, language, or workflow details that general products ignore?

## Distribution as an operating system

Examples studied: Symmetry and creator-driven mobile products.

Transferable question:
> Can acquisition become a repeatable production system rather than a one-off viral hope?

## Portfolio bootstrapping

Observed in the Clucky/Bendy/Anim8 founder context supplied during development of this skill.

Pattern:
`small/fast products → cash + audience + learning → fund/market conviction product → stronger portfolio launch capacity`

Use only with explicit role clarity and bounded experiments.
