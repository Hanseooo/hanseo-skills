# Craft Leverage

## Definition

Craft leverage is the degree to which unusually high execution quality changes whether users prefer, recommend, or pay for a product.

## High craft leverage

Often found in:
- creator tools;
- media/output tools;
- lifestyle utilities;
- personal productivity;
- consumer apps;
- visual editors;
- products with strong identity/personality;
- tools whose output is publicly shared.

## Lower visual craft leverage, higher operational craft

Examples:
- infrastructure;
- financial plumbing;
- backend APIs;
- compliance systems.

Craft still matters, but often as:
- reliability;
- correctness;
- observability;
- docs;
- predictable latency;
- error handling;
- integration quality.

## Craft dimensions

- visual coherence;
- interaction design;
- motion/sound;
- performance;
- defaults;
- output quality;
- onboarding;
- error states;
- copy;
- reliability;
- trust/transparency;
- personality;
- accessibility.

## Important warning

Craft amplifies a value proposition; it does not automatically create payment intent.

When demand is uncertain but craft leverage is high, validate with a small but genuinely polished slice rather than either:
- a fake ugly prototype that cannot demonstrate the value, or
- a fully built product before testing payment/distribution.
