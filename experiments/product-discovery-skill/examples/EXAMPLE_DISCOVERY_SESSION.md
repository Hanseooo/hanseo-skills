# Example — Blank-Slate Discovery Session

## User

I want to make my first paid independent product, but I don't have an idea yet.

## Good skill behavior

Start collaboratively rather than generating a giant list.

Example:

> Before we search for ideas, I want to calibrate the kind of bet we're making. Are you aiming for something you could plausibly ship in days/weeks, or are you comfortable with a larger multi-month product? And do you care more about getting your first strangers to pay, building something you personally love, or maximizing the chance of a durable business?
>
> While you answer, I would explore several different lanes rather than assuming a platform: recurring annoyances in tools you already use, enthusiast/desire products, transitions between popular products, communities you understand unusually well, and new platform/AI capabilities that changed what is feasible.

After receiving enough context, generate a handful of hypothesis families with different value mechanisms. Do not score them yet. Ask which ones create genuine curiosity or which recurring behaviors the user recognizes.

Research begins only when a promising direction requires current external evidence.
