# Research Mode

Research is a tool inside product discovery, not the default shape of every session.

## Trigger research when

The decision depends on fresh or external facts such as:
- exact competitors;
- current pricing;
- product launch recency;
- app/extension/plugin marketplace state;
- platform-native features;
- user reviews or complaints;
- current regulations/policies;
- public traction or founder statements;
- distribution channels;
- new technology capabilities/economics;
- recent cultural/platform shifts.

## Research intensity

### Light sanity check
Use for cheap, reversible, timing-sensitive experiments.

Goal: detect obvious blockers or exact duplicates quickly.

### Focused validation research
Use for a 1–4 week product or a meaningful commitment.

Research exact competitors, pricing, user behavior, likely distribution, and strongest substitution risk.

### Deep pressure-test
Use for expensive, operationally complex, regulated, or months-long bets.

Include:
- broad landscape;
- exact proposition search;
- user evidence;
- pricing/business models;
- platform risk;
- distribution;
- second terminology/red-team search;
- counterexamples;
- explicit unknowns.

## Exact competitor protocol

Before searching, write the exact proposition in one sentence.

Search for:
- exact workflow;
- paraphrases;
- target user terms;
- trigger terms;
- outcome terms;
- platform-specific wording;
- recent releases;
- small indie tools;
- open source;
- app stores and marketplaces;
- native platform features.

Do not search only for the broad category.

## Red-team pass

For meaningful decisions, assume the initial recommendation is wrong.

Search again using different vocabulary and sources. Try to find:
- products with fewer users;
- recent launches;
- products in adjacent geographies/languages;
- waitlists;
- free/open-source substitutes;
- native features;
- tools described differently from the proposed terminology.

If the whitespace disappears, narrow or reject the idea.

## Research output

Research should answer a decision, not maximize pages.

Return:
- what changed in the hypothesis;
- strongest supporting evidence;
- strongest disconfirming evidence;
- remaining unknowns;
- recommended next experiment.
