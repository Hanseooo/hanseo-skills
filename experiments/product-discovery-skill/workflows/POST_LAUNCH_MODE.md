# Post-Launch Mode

Once a product ships, stop treating the original idea as sacred.

## Diagnose the funnel separately

### Attention
Are people seeing it?

### Activation
Do they understand it and reach the first value moment?

### Retention
Do they return when the need repeats?

### Payment
Do activated users convert at a plausible rate?

### Economics
Do price, refunds, platform fees, support, acquisition, and variable costs make sense?

### Durability
Is performance sustained after launch novelty fades?

## Review health

Investigate negative reviews before adding features.

Classify complaints:
- reliability/crashes;
- misleading expectations;
- pricing/paywall;
- missing core behavior;
- platform incompatibility;
- onboarding/confusion;
- performance;
- trust/privacy;
- support.

A beautiful launch cannot compensate indefinitely for poor product health.

## Distribution diagnosis

If retention/payment are healthy but growth is weak, distribution may be the bottleneck.

If attention is high but payment is low, do not automatically buy more reach. Revisit proposition, audience, price, and activation.

## Iteration decisions

Choose among:
- fix quality;
- improve onboarding;
- adjust pricing/package;
- narrow audience;
- strengthen distribution;
- add requested capability;
- remove distracting capability;
- pivot mechanism;
- park/kill.

## Portfolio decision

If the product is useful but not worth long-term focus, explicitly decide whether it becomes:
- maintained cash-flow utility;
- audience/distribution asset;
- showcase;
- open-source/community asset;
- discontinued experiment.
