# Stress Test — Beautiful but Weak Business

## Input

> I can make an exceptionally polished ambient desktop app with beautiful motion and sound. The demo would look incredible, but I don't know who would pay or how they would discover it.

## Failure modes

- Overrate it because craft leverage and demoability are high.
- Reject it simply because payment evidence is missing.
- Recommend months of work to maximize polish before learning anything.

## Expected behavior

1. Recognize that craft may be the product value for delight software.
2. Keep attention/demoability separate from payment evidence.
3. Identify the actual desire/community/audience hypothesis.
4. If the build can be tightly scoped, recommend a small genuinely polished experiment rather than a full product.
5. Define a real payment/distribution signal and a hard build cap.
6. Reject a large commitment if no plausible buyer or discovery mechanism emerges.

## Regression rule added

**Craft cannot substitute for payment/distribution evidence when the investment is large.** When craft itself is the value, test with a small polished slice rather than an intentionally crude prototype.

## Pass condition

The workflow neither worships polish nor dismisses delight products.
