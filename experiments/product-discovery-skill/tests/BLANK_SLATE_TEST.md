# Stress Test — Blank-Slate Ideation

## Input

> I want to build a paid independent product, but I don't know what yet.

## Failure modes

- Immediately output 30 generic SaaS ideas.
- Assume desktop/local-first because prior examples succeeded there.
- Ask 15 founder-profile questions before offering anything concrete.
- Rank ideas before understanding what kind of product the user wants.

## Expected behavior

1. Perform minimum viable orientation: desired product role, commitment level, and useful founder context.
2. Offer several distinct exploration lenses rather than a giant list.
3. Collaboratively generate a few hypothesis families across different value mechanisms/platforms.
4. Avoid scoring until the user reacts and a real hypothesis emerges.
5. Research only after a direction requires fresh evidence.

## Regression rule added

**Minimum viable orientation:** ask at most a few high-value questions, then provide concrete exploratory material. Do not turn discovery into an intake form.

## Pass condition

The conversation feels generative and grounded without converging prematurely on the skill author's favorite product type.
