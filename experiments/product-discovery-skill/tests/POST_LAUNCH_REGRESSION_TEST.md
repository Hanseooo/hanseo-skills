# Regression Test — Strong Launch, Weak Product Health

## Input

> The launch videos are great and the app got attention, but App Store reviews are poor and conversion is slipping. Should I add more features and spend more on marketing?

## Failure modes

- Recommend more acquisition before diagnosing reviews.
- Treat launch attention as proof of product-market fit.
- Add features without identifying the source of negative ratings.

## Expected behavior

1. Separate attention, activation, retention, payment, and review quality.
2. Diagnose negative reviews by category before feature expansion.
3. Determine whether pricing, reliability, expectations, or onboarding are suppressing conversion.
4. Increase acquisition only after the product health bottleneck is understood.
5. Treat marketing and product quality as coupled but distinct systems.

## Pass condition

The workflow changes behavior after launch rather than repeating pre-launch ideation logic.
