# Evidence Model

## Traction ladder

Treat these as separate signals.

### Attention
People see, discuss, share, upvote, or react to the product.

Useful for: hook quality, cultural resonance, shareability.

Does not prove: usage, payment, retention, or business health.

### Activation
People try the product or complete the first meaningful action.

Useful for: initial comprehension and onboarding.

Does not prove: repeated need or payment.

### Retention
People come back or continue using the product when the need repeats.

Useful for: recurring value and habit.

Does not automatically prove: monetization or strong economics.

### Payment
People transact voluntarily.

Useful for: willingness to pay.

Still ask: how much, how often, acquisition source, refund rate, and whether founder/network effects distort the signal.

### Economics
Revenue supports acquisition, infrastructure, support, taxes, refunds, platform fees, and founder time at the desired scale.

### Durability
The need and reason-to-win persist after launch novelty, platform changes, and copycats.

Not every product requires high durability; short-lived asymmetric experiments can still be rational.

## Claim labels

When important, mark statements as:

- **FACT** — directly established by a reliable source or observed product behavior.
- **EVIDENCE** — a signal supporting or weakening a hypothesis.
- **INFERENCE** — a reasoned interpretation.
- **ASSUMPTION** — currently unverified but required for the thesis.
- **UNKNOWN** — explicitly unresolved.

## Evidence hierarchy for product decisions

Prefer, roughly:
1. actual payment from target users;
2. target-user behavior in a real workflow;
3. retention/repeated use;
4. direct interviews with demonstrated behavior;
5. marketplace/review/user-complaint evidence;
6. paid adjacent products;
7. searches and discussion frequency;
8. waitlists/signups;
9. likes/upvotes/viral views;
10. generic TAM reports or founder intuition alone.

Context can reorder this hierarchy. A timing product may rationally use weaker pre-launch evidence because waiting destroys the opportunity.
